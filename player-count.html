Mistfall Hunter Player Count & Stats

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

How many people play Mistfall Hunter? Here are the key statistics as of August 2026, including Steam concurrent players, global milestones, and Twitch viewership.

Global Player Milestones

Mistfall Hunter reached impressive milestones in its first week:

  • 500,000+ players - Reached in early August (first few days post-launch)
  • 1,000,000+ players - Reached August 5, 2026 (significantly boosted by Xbox Game Pass)
  • Open Beta participation - Nearly 430,000 players during Steam Next Fest (June 14-22, 2026)

Owner Estimates

Third-party tracking services estimate the following ownership numbers:

  • VG Insights: ~375,500 owners
  • PlayTracker: ~453,000 owners
  • Gamalytic: ~528,000 owners

These are estimates based on various data points and may not include Game Pass players, who don't need to purchase the game separately.

Twitch Viewership

Mistfall Hunter had a strong Twitch presence during launch week:

  • Peak Twitch viewers: 93,400 concurrent viewers
  • Twitch Drops campaigns during launch week (July 30 - August 12) drove significant viewership
  • The game maintained strong category presence through the first two weeks

Steam Review Status

As of August 2026, Steam reviews are Mixed at approximately 58-60% positive out of 7,600+ reviews. Key factors:

  • English reviews tend to be more positive, praising combat and atmosphere
  • Simplified Chinese reviews tend to be more negative, citing server stability and optimization issues
  • Common complaints: stuttering, latency, regional server issues
  • Common praise: combat feel (compared to God of War 2018), extraction tension, art direction
Data Freshness

Player count data changes daily. For the most current numbers, check SteamDB (steamdb.info/app/3282300) or activeplayer.io/mistfall-hunter. This page was last updated in August 2026.
