Mistfall Hunter Collection Pedestal Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

The Collection Pedestal is a camp building where you display your best loot as trophies — pure decoration with a "trophy wall" feel.

How to Unlock

Complete the quest "A Pearl in the Dust":

  1. Talk to Cazarro in Windrest
  2. Build the Collection Pedestal
  3. Submit required materials: Mithril Lamp, Blood Moss, Spiritwood Pin
  4. Place your first collectible

Function

  • Display your best loot as trophies in Windrest camp
  • Purely decorative — no gameplay benefits
  • Located near the campfire/tent area
Warning

Items placed on the Collection Pedestal are consumed and cannot be retrieved. Think carefully before placing rare items like Gem of the Gods.

Gyldenmist Memory Shards

Blue glowing collectibles found across Hallowgrove, Brandrgarde, and Windrest ruins:

  • Pickup binds to your account
  • Collect all fragments and submit at the Collection Pedestal
  • Unlocks backstory/lore
  • Grants crafting materials as rewards

Tips

  • Don't place your only copy of a rare item
  • Check if an item has gameplay use before displaying
  • Memory Shards are account-bound — safe to collect even if you die
  • The pedestal is near the campfire in Windrest
Summary

Unlock via "A Pearl in the Dust" quest (talk to Cazarro). Display trophies — purely decorative. Items placed are consumed! Gyldenmist Memory Shards submitted here unlock lore + materials.
