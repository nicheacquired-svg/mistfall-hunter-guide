Mistfall Hunter Codes (August 2026)

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Looking for Mistfall Hunter codes? Here's the current status as of August 2026, along with all available rewards and how to claim them.

⚠ Current Status: No Active Public Codes

As of August 1, 2026, there are no confirmed public redeem codes available for Mistfall Hunter. Any websites claiming to have working codes are unverified. Do not trust third-party code lists.

Launch Rewards (In-Game Mail)

While there are no redeem codes, all players who log in before September 1, 2026 receive launch rewards via in-game mail:

  • Thumbs Up - Emote/gesture
  • 500 Soul Coins - In-game currency
  • Ashen Cipher - Item
  • Abyssal Cipher - Item
  • Pip's Rare Pouch Voucher - Must be used within 3 days of redemption

To claim: check your in-game mail by speaking to Soekja the White Crow at camp.

Season 1 Battle Pass

The Slumbering Contract (Season 1 Battle Pass) is completely free. Players can unlock rewards by logging in for 7 cumulative days. This was confirmed in DevNote #7. No premium paid track exists - all Battle Pass rewards are earnable through play.

Loadout Share Codes

Mistfall Hunter does have loadout/build share codes - these are real and let you import other players' builds. However, these codes only import skill and talent configurations. They do not grant currency, items, or cosmetics. Be careful not to confuse loadout codes with redeem codes.

How to Redeem Codes (When Available)

Currently, Mistfall Hunter does not have a code redemption interface. If codes are added in the future, they would likely be redeemed through:

  1. The official game website (mistfallhunter.com)
  2. In-game mail system (via Soekja the White Crow at camp)
  3. Potential future redemption page on the official site

Rewards from official events are distributed directly to in-game mail, not through code entry.

Partner Events

Partners like LagoFast have occasionally run giveaway events. These are time-limited and require following specific partner instructions. Always verify through the official Mistfall Hunter Discord or social media before participating.

Where to Watch for New Codes

To catch new codes or rewards the moment they drop:

  • Official Discord (discord.gg/mistfallhunter) - DevNote announcements
  • Official X/Twitter (@MistfallHunter)
  • Steam News page for the game
  • Official website (mistfallhunter.com) news section
Key Takeaway

No public redeem codes exist right now. Claim your launch rewards by logging in before September 1. The Season 1 Battle Pass is free with 7-day login. Loadout share codes are for builds only, not rewards.
