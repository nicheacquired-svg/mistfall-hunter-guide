Mistfall Hunter Withered Knight Build

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

The Withered Knight is a heavy frontline brawler with two playstyles: Greatsword (Wither mark + detonation burst) and Polearm & Shield (suppression and team defense).

Greatsword vs Polearm & Shield

  • Greatsword — Wither mechanic: apply marks via charged attacks/skills, then detonate for massive delayed burst.
  • Polearm & Shield — Suppression playstyle with gap-closing, crowd control, team defense. See our Spear Shield Build for dedicated guide.

Greatsword Build (Wither Mechanic)

Core Mechanic: Wither

  • Charged light/heavy attacks apply Wither marks
  • "Wither Imprint" skill applies marks in AOE
  • Parry counterattacks also apply marks
  • Settlement attack detonates all marks for burst damage

Talents

  • Valor — Wither detonation damage
  • Burst — Settlement attack enhancement
  • Vitality — Survivability
  • Elusive — Dodge between mark application and detonation

Affix Priority

  1. Aegis — Defense and physical resistance
  2. Bulwark — Block damage reduction and energy
  3. Tenacious — Health and healing
  4. Valor — Wither detonation damage
  5. Burst — Burst windows
  6. Elusive — Mobility

Gameplay Tips

  • Stack then detonate — Build 3+ Wither stacks before settling
  • Use terrain — No ranged options; need corners to close distance
  • Parry applies marks — Practice against PvE mobs first
  • Be the anchor in trios — Hold frontline, zone enemies

Strengths & Weaknesses

  • Pros: Highest frontline durability, Wither burst, team anchor, can revive (Polearm)
  • Cons: No ranged, vulnerable to kiting, complex mechanic, slow
Build Summary

Greatsword for Wither burst, Polearm & Shield for team suppression. Prioritize Aegis and Bulwark. Not for beginners due to Wither complexity. Best paired with Seer healer and ranged DPS.
