Mistfall Hunter PvP Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

PvP is not a separate mode — it's woven into the core extraction gameplay. Every run, you may encounter rival Gyldhunters.

How PvP Works

  • Up to 15 players per match
  • Teams of up to 3; 4 players can split into two teams
  • No pure PvE mode — always be prepared

Death Rules

QueueOn DownLoot
SoloImmediate executionAll items dropped; lootable
TrioDowned; can be revivedIf executed, all dropped; WK's Intervene can revive

No Soul Form in PvP — unlike PvE deaths, PvP deaths are permanent.

Tactics

  • Never fight fair — If outnumbered, run
  • Third-party fights — Let two teams fight, clean up survivors
  • Zone control — Traps, Impact Grenades, Mud Jars
  • Silence Dust — Disables enemy abilities
  • Lure Jar — Draws enemies into ambushes
  • Purple gear — High stats but high risk if you die

Best PvP Classes

See Best PvP Class for details. Quick: T0 Shadowstrix, T0.5 Blackarrow, T1 Sorcerer.

Training

Press H in camp → Training Record → PvP tutorial.

Balance (August 2026)

  • Blackarrow escape skill cost increased (nerf)
  • Sorcerer buffed
  • Withered Knight adjustments
Summary

PvP is core to extraction, not separate. Solo = permanent death. Trio = revivable. Use tactical items. Shadowstrix and Blackarrow top picks. Practice in Training Record.
