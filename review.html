Mistfall Hunter Review

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Is Mistfall Hunter worth playing? Here's a comprehensive breakdown of critical reception, user reviews, and platform-specific impressions as of August 2026.

Overall Verdict

Mistfall Hunter sits at approximately 7 to 7.5 out of 10 across media reviews. The game launched to a mixed but generally positive critical reception, with strong praise for its combat system and dark fantasy atmosphere, offset by criticism of server stability and technical optimization.

Critical Reception

As of August 2026, Metacritic and OpenCritic have not yet published aggregate scores for Mistfall Hunter. Individual media impressions include:

  • PC Gamer compared the combat favorably to God of War (2018), praising the weight and feel of melee combat
  • Complete Xbox gave the game an 80% score, noting it as a strong Xbox offering
  • GameCritix and WayTooManyGames published generally positive reviews highlighting the extraction tension
  • Rectify Gaming provided a balanced review covering both strengths and weaknesses

Steam User Reviews

Steam reviews are Mixed at approximately 58% positive out of 6,900+ reviews. The split is notable:

  • English-language reviews skew positive, with players praising combat, class variety, and the extraction loop
  • Simplified Chinese reviews skew negative, primarily citing server stability, high latency to Chinese servers, and optimization issues
  • Common positive themes: combat feel, dark fantasy atmosphere, class depth, no pay-to-win
  • Common negative themes: stuttering, server disconnects, balance issues, Brandrgarde solo mode absence

Platform-Specific Impressions

Xbox

The Xbox Store reviews show a polarized audience - approximately half of Xbox reviews are 1-star, primarily due to network issues during the launch period. However, players who got past the server issues reported a positive experience, especially through Game Pass where the financial barrier is lower.

PS5

PS5 players benefit from PS5 Pro Enhanced support, offering higher rendering resolution and more stable frame rates. The base PS5 experience is decent but less crisp. DualSense haptic feedback and adaptive triggers are not supported at launch but are confirmed for a future update. PS Plus is required for online play.

PC

PC offers the most customizable experience. Recommended GPU is RTX 2070 or RX 5700 XT. Common issues include stuttering and frame pacing problems that the developers are actively investigating. There is no FOV slider - 21:9 ultrawide is the only way to expand your field of view.

What Critics Praise

  • Combat system - Weighty, responsive melee with meaningful class differentiation
  • Extraction tension - The risk-reward of losing everything creates genuine adrenaline
  • Art direction - Dark fantasy aesthetic with atmospheric mist effects
  • Class variety - 12 distinct playstyles across 6 classes with dual stances
  • No pay-to-win - All microtransactions are cosmetic only

What Critics Criticize

  • Server stability - Disconnects and latency issues, especially in certain regions
  • Optimization - Stuttering and frame pacing problems on all platforms
  • Balance - Some classes feel stronger than others (being addressed with patches)
  • Lack of solo Brandrgarde - The harder map doesn't support solo play yet (coming September)
Bottom Line

Mistfall Hunter is a promising extraction ARPG with excellent combat and atmosphere, hampered by launch-period technical issues. If you enjoy extraction games and dark fantasy, it's worth trying - especially through Game Pass. The developers are actively patching, so expect improvements.
