Mistfall Hunter Max Level & Progression

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Multiple progression systems work in parallel in Mistfall Hunter. Here is the level cap, the full XP curve, and what matters after you max out.

Max Level: 12

The character level cap is Level 12 - the same for all six classes. Reaching it costs 14,400 total XP and completes your Talent progression with your 8th and final Talent Point. The cap is visible in-game through the trophy for reaching maximum level, and no class or build raises it.

Key Level Unlocks

  • Level 3: Auction House access opens
  • Level 5: Camp Revitalization, Forge, Workshop, and Scavenger Squad
  • Level 6: Camp upgrade system and the Path of Glory ranked ladder
  • Level 9: Gyldenblod Pool upgrade tier
  • Level 10: Most classes complete their core skill unlocks
  • Level 12: Final Talent Point and full ability progression

The One-Level-Per-Raid Rule

You cannot bank more than one level per run. Once you earn enough XP to finish the level you entered with, the run stops paying out - kills, chests, and the escape bonus all stop counting, and overflow XP is lost. Treat leveling up mid-raid as your signal to extract immediately.

Fastest XP Sources

  • Escaping and the survival bonus are guaranteed - everything else scales with difficulty and party size
  • Solo runs pay roughly 3x per kill versus a trio; Chaos and Cataclysm variants pay about 30% more than Normal
  • Mist Lord bosses: up to 260 XP solo (80 in a trio) - the biggest single payout once you are geared
  • AI Hunters pay the same XP as real player kills without the unpredictability
  • Chests: 15–60 XP solo depending on tier (half that in trios)

Spirit Infusion: Skip the Grind

Once one character reaches Level 12 and your Gyldenblod Pool hits level 4, talk to Dew and select Spirit Infusion: paying 3,600 Gyldenblod instantly boosts any new character to Level 12 with the full talent budget. Raising the Pool to level 9 doubles the rate, halving the cost to 1,800. This is the efficient path to maxing all six classes - see Change Class guide for character slots.

Talent System

  • 8 Talent Points total, arriving at levels 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, and 12
  • Free respec at low levels
  • Free class change at low levels — experiment
  • Skills tied to weapon stances, not character level

Camp Building Upgrades

  • Warehouse (priority #1)
  • Gyldenblod Pool, Workshop, Goddess Statue, Shop, Tavern, Auction House
  • Maxing all 7 = achievement

Gem of the Gods

  • Warehouse Lv11-13 needs 3 total
  • Goddess Statue Lv8 needs 1
  • From high-difficulty content

Endgame Goals (from achievements)

  • Extract Holy item
  • Defeat Mist Lord on Einherjar
  • Extract Gem of the Gods
  • Max class level
  • Max all 7 camp buildings
Summary

Level 12 is the cap for every class: 14,400 XP, 8 Talent Points, and all core systems unlocked by Level 6-10. One level per raid max - extract when you level up. Solo pays ~3x trio XP; Spirit Infusion skips the grind for alts. After the cap, progression shifts to gear, camp, and Path of Glory.
