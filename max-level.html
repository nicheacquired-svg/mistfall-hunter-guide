Mistfall Hunter Max Level & Progression

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Multiple progression systems work in parallel in Mistfall Hunter.

Progression Systems

SystemDescription
Character LevelMain progression; unlocks talents
Class LevelEach of 6 classes levels independently
Camp Buildings7 buildings, multiple upgrade levels each
Season PassSlumbering Contract (free); login 7 days

Talent System

  • Talents unlock with character level
  • Free respec at low levels
  • Free class change at low levels — experiment
  • Skills tied to weapon stances, not character level

Camp Building Upgrades

  • Warehouse (priority #1)
  • Gyldenblod Pool, Workshop, Goddess Statue, Shop, Tavern, Auction House
  • Maxing all 7 = achievement

Gem of the Gods

  • Warehouse Lv11-13 needs 3 total
  • Goddess Statue Lv8 needs 1
  • From high-difficulty content

Endgame Goals (from achievements)

  • Extract Holy item
  • Defeat Mist Lord on Einherjar
  • Extract Gem of the Gods
  • Max class level
  • Max all 7 camp buildings
Note

Exact level cap not officially published. Check MistfallDB for community-verified info.

Summary

Parallel progression: character, class, camp buildings, season pass. Free respecs/class changes at low levels. 7 buildings to max. Endgame: Holy items, Mist Lord Einherjar, Gem of Gods. Exact cap not confirmed.
