Mistfall Hunter PvE Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

No pure PvE mode exists — official store confirms this. PvE content refers to bosses, monsters, and environmental challenges in the PvPvE world.

Understanding PvE

  • Boss fights (Mist Lord, General Harald, Greataxe Berserker)
  • Map monsters (Corroded, Harpies, Returner Woodling)
  • Environmental hazards (Gyldenmist shrinking zone)
  • Collectibles (Ciphers, Gyldenmist Memory Shards)

Mist Lord (Ultimate Challenge)

  • Bosses: Otto the Flesheater + Harald the Fallen General (same arena)
  • Enrage: 40% HP triggers major mechanic
  • Safe loot window: 45 seconds after boss death
  • Einherjar difficulty: Requires Dried Flower Knot; drops Holy weapons

Flesheater Attacks

AttackCounter
PounceDodge sideways
BerserkSurvive; wait it out
Ground SmashJump or dodge away
Gyldenblod PukeStay behind or to the side

Recommended Team

  • Tank: Withered Knight or Mercenary
  • DPS: Sorcerer (AOE) or Blackarrow (ranged)
  • Support: Seer (Reverent) — essential for Einherjar

Holy Weapons

  • Each boss drops one unique Holy weapon
  • From Einherjar difficulty Mist Lord encounters
  • Used for forging high-tier gear

Other Encounters

  • Returner Woodling — Kills for Soul of Return (extraction key)
  • Greataxe Berserker — Hallowgrove hidden boss
  • Soulgnawer — New boss (August 6)

Tips

  • Watch for PvP during boss fights — most vulnerable moment
  • Use 45-second loot window quickly
  • DPS/support should never pull boss aggro
  • Save escape tools for PvP ambushes
Summary

No pure PvE mode. Mist Lord is ultimate challenge (Flesheater + Harald, 40% enrage, 45s loot window). Team: Tank + DPS + Seer. Holy weapons from Einherjar. Always watch for PvP.
