Mistfall Hunter Beta Testing History

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Before its July 2026 launch, Mistfall Hunter went through three beta phases that shaped the final game.

Beta Timeline

PhaseDateKey Additions
Beta 2April 2025Small invite test; core loop validation
Beta 3September 2025The Camp hub, female variants, Chaos mode, General Harald
Open Beta 4June 14-22, 2026Steam Next Fest; all platforms; ~430K participants

Open Beta 4 Details

  • Platforms: Steam, Xbox, PS5 simultaneously
  • Peak concurrent: 16,683 on Steam
  • Total participants: ~430,000
  • Data wipe: All progress wiped; only cosmetics carried over
  • Feedback: Camp optimization, combat rework (abandoned energy-fatigue for free-flowing combat)

Beta to Launch Changes

  • Combat rework — Energy-fatigue replaced with Breakaway system
  • Optimization — Camp frame rate issues addressed
  • "The Camp" evolved into "Windrest"

As of August 2026, no active demo exists. Xbox Game Pass subscribers can play the full game.

Summary

3 beta phases: Beta 2 (Apr 2025), Beta 3 (Sep 2025), Open Beta 4 (Jun 2026, ~430K). Open Beta led to major combat rework. No active demo post-launch.
