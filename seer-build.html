Mistfall Hunter Best Seer Build

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

The Seer is Mistfall Hunter's only support class, with two paths: Reverent (healing/shielding) and Blasphemer (curses/self-sustain). The Seer excels in trio play but struggles in solo queues.

Reverent vs Blasphemer

  • Reverent — Team healer and shielder. Restore health, provide shields, control crowds. Essential for trio PvE like Mist Lord fights.
  • Blasphemer — Curse-based DPS with self-sustain through Zeal. Strong against bosses like General Harald. More self-sufficient but loses team utility.

Reverent Build (Healing Support)

Recommended Skills

  • Punishment Rune — Basic offensive rune
  • Burst Rune — AOE damage for clearing adds
  • Intimidation Rune — Debuffs enemies
  • Wind Surge (Divine Art) — AOE knockback to peel enemies
  • Healing Art (Divine Art) — Primary team heal; Seer's signature

Recommended Talents

  • Blessing — Enhances healing output
  • Spirit Shield — Improves shield effectiveness
  • Brotherhood — Extends buffs to nearby allies
  • Eloquence — Cooldown reduction

Blasphemer Build (Curse DPS)

Builds curse power through attacks, entering Zeal state that boosts damage and provides self-healing.

Recommended Talents

  • Stoic — Damage reduction while channeling
  • Valor — Raw damage increase
  • Seamless — Ability flow improvements

Affix Priority

  1. Eloquence — Cooldown reduction
  2. Blessing — Support ability enhancement
  3. Spirit Shield — Shield effectiveness
  4. Brotherhood — Team buff extension
  5. Seamless — Utility
  6. Stoic — Damage reduction (Blasphemer)

Team Composition

  • Ideal trio: Withered Knight/Mercenary (tank) + Sorcerer/Blackarrow (DPS) + Seer (healer)
  • Communication is key — Time heals with ally push/retreat calls
  • Position behind the tank — Never frontline as a Seer
  • Save Healing Art for critical moments — Use at ~40% HP, not for chip damage
Build Summary

Reverent for trio PvE, Blasphemer for solo/boss fights. Prioritize Eloquence and Blessing. Weakest solo class but invaluable in organized teams. Not recommended for beginners.
