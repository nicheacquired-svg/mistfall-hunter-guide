Mistfall Hunter Best PvP Class

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

PvP is the heart of Mistfall Hunter's extraction gameplay — every run, you risk your loot against other players. Here's the consensus PvP tier list.

PvP Class Tier List

TierClassPvP Strength
T0Shadowstrix (Dual Blades)Stealth burst; pick targets, engage on own terms, disengage at will
T0.5BlackarrowRanged suppression: Barbed Arrow + Bleed + Impact Grenades
T1SorcererAOE burst in trio fights; devastating if protected
T1.5Withered Knight (P&S)Team anchor; can revive with Intervene; vulnerable to kiting
T2MercenaryDurable but low ceiling; outclassed by WK in trios
T2SeerTeam healer; helpless in 1v1

Why Shadowstrix Dominates PvP

  • Engagement control — Stealth decides when/where to fight
  • Burst damage — Flash Stride + Shadow Strike deletes squishy targets
  • Escape tools — Smoke Bomb and Flash Stride for reliable disengage

Blackarrow: The Ranged Menace

  • Barbed Arrow applies Bleed — forces disengage
  • Impact Grenades for crowd control
  • Traps secure flanks and escape routes
  • Note: August 6 patch increased escape skill cost (slight nerf)

PvP Death Rules

  • Solo: Downed = immediate execution. No revive.
  • Trio: Downed = can be revived. WK's Intervene revives during combat.
  • Loot: PvP death drops all carried items.
  • No Soul Form: PvP deaths don't grant Soul Form recovery.

Training

Press H in camp → Training Record → PvP tutorial. Practice matchups before live matches.

Summary

Shadowstrix (Dual Blades) is the PvP king. Blackarrow top ranged. Sorcerer dominates trio fights if protected. Balance patches shift meta — stay updated.
