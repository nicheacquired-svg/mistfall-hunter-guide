Mistfall Hunter Tier List (August 2026)

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

This tier list reflects community consensus from multiple sources including IGN Wiki, fandomwire, and mmoexp as of August 2026. Mistfall Hunter has no official tier list - rankings are based on community analysis and are subject to change with balance patches.

Important Note

Different sources have different rankings. This is a synthesized view. Balance patches (especially the Aug 6 update that buffed Sorcerer and Withered Knight) shift the meta regularly. Tier lists reflect opinions, not absolute truth.

Trio Tier List (3-Player Squads)

In trio play, team composition matters more than individual class strength. A balanced squad typically includes a frontline (Mercenary/Withered Knight), a damage dealer (Sorcerer/Blackarrow), and a support (Seer).

S
Sorcerer · Blackarrow — Highest damage output and ranged pressure define the trio meta
A
Seer (Reverent) · Shadowstrix (Dual Blades) · Mercenary (S&S) · Withered Knight (P&S) — Strong picks with defined team roles
B
Mercenary (Warhammer) · WK (Greatsword) · Shadowstrix (Dagger) · Seer (Blasphemer) — Situational or lower-ceiling alternatives

Solo Tier List

Solo play favors classes with self-sustain, escape tools, and 1v1 power. The ability to disengage and extract safely is paramount.

S
Shadowstrix (Dual Blades) · Blackarrow — Stealth and range provide the safest solo experience
A
Mercenary · Withered Knight · Sorcerer — Viable with good play; Sorcerer needs careful positioning
B
Seer — Weakest solo; designed for team support, struggles alone

How Rankings Work

These rankings synthesize multiple community sources:

  • IGN Wiki ranks from a trio perspective: S = Sorcerer, Blackarrow; A = Seer, Shadowstrix; B = Withered Knight, Mercenary
  • fandomwire (August): 1. Sorcerer → 2. Shadowstrix → 3. Blackarrow → 4. Mercenary → 5. Withered Knight → 6. Seer
  • mmoexp (Trio): S = Blackarrow, Sorcerer, Seer (Reverent); A = Shadowstrix (Dual Blades), Mercenary (S&S), WK (P&S)
  • mmoexp (Solo): S = Shadowstrix (Dual Blades), Blackarrow; A = Mercenary, Withered Knight, Sorcerer; B = Seer

Consensus Takeaways

Across all sources, several points are consistent:

  • Ranged classes (Sorcerer, Blackarrow) and stealth burst (Shadowstrix) are consistently top-tier
  • Mercenary is always recommended as the best beginner class despite mid-tier ranking
  • Seer has the highest trio value but lowest solo performance
  • The August 6 balance patch buffed Sorcerer and Withered Knight while adjusting Blackarrow's escape skill cost
  • No class is unplayable - skill and game knowledge matter more than tier placement
Disclaimer

This is not an official ranking. The meta will continue to evolve with patches. Use this as a starting point, not gospel. The best class is the one you enjoy and play well.
