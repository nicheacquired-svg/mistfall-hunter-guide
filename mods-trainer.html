Mistfall Hunter Mods & Trainer Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

No official mod support. Community trainers/cheat tables exist but carry significant risks.

Warning

Using trainers in public matches = permanent ban. Family Share violations ban both accounts.

Available Tools (not linked for safety)

  • Trainers — Standalone programs with hotkey cheats
  • Cheat Engine Tables (.CT) — 48+ options
  • Features: Infinite health, stamina, one-hit kill, infinite resources, super speed, no cooldown, FOV, item editing

Usage Risk

  • Solo/private: Lower risk (still against ToS)
  • Public matchmaking: Near-certain permanent ban

Anti-Cheat

  • Bellring proprietary anti-cheat
  • Detects memory modification, DLL injection, process interference
  • Family Share: Both accounts permanently banned
  • Steam Deck/Linux: Proton 11 works; anti-cheat doesn't block gameplay

What NOT to Do

  • Download DLL files (malware/detected)
  • Disable anti-cheat (instant ban)
  • Modify config files for advantages
  • Use FOV tools (see Technical page)

Safe Alternatives

  • Read our Beginner Guide
  • Use recommended builds
  • Practice in Training Record (press H in camp)
Summary

No official mods. Trainers in public = permanent ban. Don't download DLLs or disable anti-cheat. Use guides and builds instead.
