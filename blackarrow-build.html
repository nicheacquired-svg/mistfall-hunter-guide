Mistfall Hunter Blackarrow Build

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Blackarrow is the ranged archer — high-risk, high-reward DPS who excels at kiting. Lowest health alongside Shadowstrix; relies on distance and traps.

Sky Piercer Build (S-Tier)

Skills

  • Barbed Arrow — Applies Bleed; core pressure tool
  • Bloodfly Arrow — Additional DoT
  • Steel Arrow — Reliable single-target
  • Scattershot — AOE option
  • Impact Grenade — Crowd control; knockback
  • Sky Piercer — Signature burst
  • Seeker — Tracking arrow
  • Placement Trap — Pre-combat zone control

Talents

  • Seeker — Tracking and accuracy
  • Seamless — Sustained output
  • Valor — Raw damage
  • Elusive — Mobility and dodge

Affix Priority

  1. Seeker — Accuracy critical for ranged
  2. Seamless — Sustained damage
  3. Valor — Damage
  4. Elusive — Mobility
  5. Fervor — Attack speed

Strategy

  1. Pre-combat: Place traps; secure escape route
  2. Engage at maximum range
  3. Maintain distance; Impact Grenade if approached
  4. Apply Bleed with Barbed Arrow
  5. Reposition after kills — never stay still

Balance Status

  • August 6: Escape skill cost increased (nerf)
  • Tier controversy: S-tier or weakest depending on source
  • Season 2: New Javelin weapon coming
Summary

Sky Piercer is top build. Seeker + Seamless affixes. Core: traps → range → maintain distance → knockback if approached → reposition. Fragile but powerful at range.
