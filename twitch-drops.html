Mistfall Hunter Twitch Drops Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Mistfall Hunter ran two rounds of Twitch Drops during its launch week, offering exclusive in-game cosmetics for watching partnered streams. Here's a complete guide to how the drops worked, what rewards were available, and how to claim future drops.

Current Status

As of mid-August 2026, there are no active Twitch Drops campaigns. Both launch rounds have ended. Check the official drops page (mistfallhunter.com/drops) for future campaigns.

How Twitch Drops Work

Twitch Drops are in-game rewards earned by watching designated Twitch streams. Here's the process:

  1. Link your accounts: Visit the official Mistfall Hunter drops page and connect your platform account (Steam/PSN/Xbox) to your Twitch account
  2. Watch Drops Enabled streams: Find Twitch streams with the "Drops Enabled" tag for Mistfall Hunter
  3. Accumulate watch time: Different rewards unlock at different watch time thresholds
  4. Claim on Twitch: Rewards appear in your Twitch inventory - claim within 24 hours
  5. Collect in-game: Rewards arrive via in-game mail - speak to Soekja the White Crow at camp to collect

The Legendary Celestial Mistveil Outfit was the headline reward of Round 2, with a very rare 0.1% drop rate making it highly coveted.

How to Claim Drops

  1. After earning a drop, go to your Twitch Inventory (twitch.tv/inventory)
  2. Click "Claim" on each earned reward within 24 hours of earning it
  3. Launch Mistfall Hunter on your linked platform
  4. Visit Soekja the White Crow at your camp (Windrest)
  5. Check your in-game mail to receive the items

Troubleshooting

Common issues with Twitch Drops:

  • Drop not appearing in-game: Ensure your accounts are properly linked. Re-link if necessary. Items may take up to 24 hours to arrive in-game after claiming on Twitch.
  • Watch time not counting: Make sure the stream has the "Drops Enabled" tag. Not all Mistfall Hunter streams have drops enabled.
  • Can't find Soekja: Soekja the White Crow is located at the Windrest camp, near the mail area.

Future Drops

While no Twitch Drops campaigns are currently active, the developers may run new campaigns for seasonal events or major content updates. To stay informed:

  • Check mistfallhunter.com/drops regularly
  • Follow the official Discord announcements channel
  • Watch for DevNote posts that typically precede new campaigns
Quick Reference

Link accounts → Watch Drops Enabled streams → Claim on Twitch within 24h → Talk to Soekja in-game. Both rounds have ended; watch for future campaigns.
