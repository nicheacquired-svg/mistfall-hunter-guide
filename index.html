About Mistfall Hunter

Mistfall Hunter is a dark fantasy PvPvE extraction ARPG developed by Bellring Games and published by Skystone Games. Released on July 29, 2026 for PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, with day-one Xbox Game Pass support.

Players take on the role of Gyldhunters, venturing into a world consumed by the Gyldenmist - a deadly fog born from fallen gods' corrupted blood. Your mission: collect Gyldenblood, fight Corroded monsters and rival Gyldhunters, defeat the Returner Woodling, and extract with the Soul of Return. Die, and you lose everything you carried.

The game features 6 unique classes, each with dual weapon stances (12 total playstyles), full cross-platform multiplayer for up to 3-player squads, and two maps with distinct extraction mechanics. No pay-to-win mechanics - all microtransactions are cosmetic only.
