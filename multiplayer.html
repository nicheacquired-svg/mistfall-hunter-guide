Mistfall Hunter Multiplayer Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Mistfall Hunter is built around squad-based PvPvE extraction: you fight monsters, rival hunters, and the clock together. This guide covers squad sizes, matchmaking, how to add friends, and the region rules nobody explains in-game.

Multiplayer Basics

Solo, Trios & the Missing Duo Queue

There are two queues: Solo and Trios. If you queue as a pair, you enter the trio pool - you can disable Fill Party to avoid a random third, but you can still run into full three-player squads and fight outnumbered. Bellring Games said in a community AMA that a permanent duo queue is not planned in the short term, to protect matchmaking pool size and balance.

Four friends want to play together? Split into two pairs or a pair-plus-one: both squads can queue the same region simultaneously and may land in the same match - as separate, hostile teams.

How to Unlock Multiplayer & Add Friends

Multiplayer unlocks through normal progression:

  1. Complete the tutorial (including the scripted troll fight you are meant to lose)
  2. Survive your first solo Hallowgrove run and return to camp
  3. The Friends menu (top-left of the hub screen) unlocks

Adding teammates:

  • Friends are added by in-game Player ID - not your Steam or Xbox username
  • Open the Teams menu (arrow icon next to your username) → Friends List → search by Player ID
  • The Recruitment Hall lets you post squad recruitment by map, class, and difficulty
  • Enable Fill Party to let matchmaking backfill a random third player

⚠️ Crossplay Is Not Cross-Region

This is the single biggest gotcha in Mistfall Hunter multiplayer:

  • Crossplay: PC (Steam and Xbox app), PS5, and Xbox Series X|S share one matchmaking pool
  • Cross-region: Not supported. Your store account's registered region decides your servers

Five server regions exist: China, US, Germany, Singapore, and Brazil. There are no Oceania servers (a community vote for one is being organized). North American accounts (US, Canada, Mexico, and certain other NA countries) get the NA build and can only connect to US servers - a US player and a UK player cannot join the same run, even on the same platform. Matchmaking & Bots

Matchmaking is automatic - there is no server browser. Bots are part of the matchmaking pool long-term (per the developer AMA), with AI improvements planned over time. Matchmaking can run slow at off-peak hours or for pairs in the trio pool; requeue if stuck. One warning for extraction runs: a disconnect can register as a death and cost your loot, so avoid queuing on an unstable connection.

Finding Teammates (LFG)

  • Official Discord (discord.gg/mistfallhunter) - the main LFG hub, organized by region
  • r/MistfallHunter on Reddit - regular LFG threads around bosses and events
  • In-game Recruitment Hall - post your own squad listing
