Mistfall Hunter Release Date & Price

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Mistfall Hunter was released on July 29, 2026 for PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, with day-one Xbox Game Pass support. Here's everything about the release, pricing, and editions.

Release Date Details

  • Steam release: July 29, 2026 at 18:00 PT (UTC July 30, 01:00)
  • Global release: July 30, 2026 (all platforms unlock)
  • Platforms: PC (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X|S - simultaneous launch
  • No pre-load was available at launch; download started at release (~39GB download, ~77.8GB disk space)

The game was originally planned for an earlier release but was delayed to synchronize the PS5 launch and overhaul the combat system (replacing the old "energy-fatigue" mechanic with the current free-flow combat).

Launch Discount

A 10% launch discount was available on all platforms until August 12, 2026 (Standard dropped to ~$22.49). The Deluxe upgrade was also discounted to $13.49 during this period.

Deluxe Edition Contents

The Deluxe Edition includes everything in Standard plus:

  • Forsaken Skeleton - Legendary cosmetic outfit
  • Web of Fate Earring - Cosmetic accessory
  • Shipwreck Coast - Background/camp scene
  • Justice - Standing pose
  • Pip Emote Pack - Emotes
  • Dew Avatar - Profile avatar
  • Light Feather Frame - Avatar frame
  • 2,500 Fate Coins - Premium currency (cosmetic only)

Standard edition owners can upgrade to Deluxe at any time. Deluxe rewards can only be claimed once per account.

Xbox Game Pass

Mistfall Hunter launched on Xbox Game Pass day one, available to both Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass subscribers at no additional cost. This was a major factor in the game reaching 1 million+ players within its first week.

Game Pass subscribers also get access to Xbox Cloud Gaming, allowing play on mobile devices and browsers. Xbox Play Anywhere support means progress syncs between Xbox consoles and PC.

No Pay-to-Win

The developers have confirmed that Mistfall Hunter has no pay-to-win mechanics. Fate Coins (premium currency) can only purchase cosmetic items. All gameplay-affecting content is earned through play. The Steam store page explicitly states zero paid stat advantages.

Beta Timeline

Before the full release, Mistfall Hunter went through several beta phases:

  • Beta 2: April 2025
  • Beta 3: September 2025 (added The Camp hub, female class variants, Chaos mode, General Harald boss)
  • Open Beta 4: June 14-22, 2026 (Steam Next Fest, ~430,000 participants, data wiped, only cosmetics carried over)
Quick Summary

Out now: July 29, 2026 on PC/PS5/Xbox. Standard $24.99, Deluxe $39.99. On Game Pass day one. No pay-to-win. No cross-save except Xbox Play Anywhere.
