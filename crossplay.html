Mistfall Hunter Crossplay Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Yes, Mistfall Hunter supports full cross-platform play across PC (Steam and Xbox App), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S from launch day. Here's everything you need to know about playing with friends on different platforms.

Cross-Platform Support

All platforms share the same multiplayer servers. PC, PS5, and Xbox players can queue together in the same matches, form squads, and interact in-game. Crossplay is enabled by default - no toggle needed.

Match sizes support up to 15 players per session, with squads of up to 3 players. Four friends can split into two squads and end up in the same server for cross-squad play.

⚠ No Cross-Save

Cross-platform play is supported, but cross-platform progress/save sharing is NOT. Your progress is locked to the platform you play on. The only exception is Xbox Play Anywhere, which shares progress between Xbox consoles and PC.

How to Add Friends

Adding cross-platform friends requires the in-game friend system:

  1. Complete the tutorial and your first Hallowgrove extraction to unlock the friend menu
  2. Open the friend menu and search by Player ID (not platform-specific username)
  3. Send a friend request - once accepted, you can invite them to your squad

You cannot add friends through Steam, PSN, or Xbox Live directly - only through the in-game Player ID system.

Server Regions

Mistfall Hunter has 5 server regions:

RegionLocation
ChinaAsia
United StatesNorth America
GermanyEurope
SingaporeSouth-East Asia
BrazilSouth America

There is currently no Oceania server. The community is organizing a vote for an OCE server. Players in Australia/New Zealand typically connect to Singapore or US servers with higher latency.

⚠ Region Locking

North American accounts are locked to North American servers and can only download the NA version of the game. This is by design. Choose your region carefully when creating your account.

Platform Requirements

Online play requires different subscriptions depending on your platform:

  • PC (Steam): No subscription required. Free online play.
  • PS5: Requires PS Plus subscription (any tier)
  • Xbox: Requires Game Pass Core or Ultimate

Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass subscribers can play Mistfall Hunter at no additional purchase cost - it's included as a day-one Game Pass title.

Xbox Play Anywhere

Mistfall Hunter supports Xbox Play Anywhere, meaning if you own the game on Xbox, you can also play it on PC at no extra cost, and your progress syncs between Xbox consoles and PC. This is the only cross-platform progress sharing available.

Summary

Full crossplay: PC + PS5 + Xbox. No cross-save (except Xbox Play Anywhere). Add friends by Player ID after first extraction. 5 server regions, region-locked accounts. PS5 needs PS Plus, Xbox needs Game Pass Core.
