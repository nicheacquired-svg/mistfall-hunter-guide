Mistfall Hunter PS5 & PS5 Pro Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

PS5 launch simultaneous with PC and Xbox. PS5 Pro officially Enhanced — the most polished console version.

PS5 Pro Enhanced

  • Higher rendering resolution
  • More stable frame rate
  • "Most refined console version" per reviewers

Performance vs Quality Mode

ModeResolutionFrame Rate
PerformanceLower~60fps (locked)
Quality4K targetUnstable (can drop below 48fps)
PS5 BaseLower in bothVaries; softer than Pro
Important

DualSense haptic feedback and adaptive triggers NOT supported at launch. Confirmed for future update.

PS Plus Requirements

  • Online multiplayer: Requires PS Plus (Essential or higher)
  • Game is always-online (no offline mode)
  • Not included in PS Plus game catalog — purchase separately

Cross-Platform

  • Full crossplay with PC and Xbox from day one
  • Add friends via Player ID
  • Region-locked: NA accounts connect to NA servers only
  • Servers: China, US, Germany, Singapore, Brazil (no Oceania)
Summary

PS5 Pro Enhanced. Performance mode ~60fps recommended for PvP. DualSense coming later. PS Plus required for online. Full crossplay. Base PS5 playable but softer.
