Mistfall Hunter Cipher Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Ciphers are puzzle collectibles scattered across Mistfall Hunter's maps. Solving them reveals lore and grants rewards.

What Are Ciphers?

Glowing glyph puzzles found in Hallowgrove, Brandrgarde, and Windrest ruins. Solving provides:

  • Lore fragments — Backstory about fallen gods and Gyldenmist
  • Rewards — Crafting materials, Soul Coins, rare items
  • Quest progression — Some tie into quest lines like "A Pearl in the Dust"

Cipher Types

  • Golden Cipher — Higher tier rewards; found in dangerous areas
  • Seal Cipher — Requires specific items to unlock
  • Ashen Cipher — Launch reward (in-game mail before September 1)
  • Abyssal Cipher — Launch reward (in-game mail before September 1)

How to Solve

  1. Symbol matching — Align rotating glyphs to match a pattern
  2. Sequence puzzles — Activate symbols in correct order
  3. Item requirements — Some need specific items (like Dried Flower Knot)
  4. Environmental clues — Look for nearby inscriptions

Locations

  • Hallowgrove — Easier ciphers for beginners; in ruins and near boss arenas
  • Brandrgarde — Higher tier in Iron and Fire questline area and Forbidden Zone
  • Windrest ruins — Some in hub camp's ruined sections
Note

Work in progress. For latest community-documented solutions, check the IGN Wiki and MistfallDB.

Tips

  • Explore thoroughly — ciphers hidden in corners and behind objects
  • Bring a friend — while solving, you're vulnerable to PvP
  • Check after patches — new ciphers may be added
  • Gyldenmist Memory Shards are separate but also found on maps
Summary

Ciphers are puzzle collectibles rewarding lore and items. Found in ruins on both maps. Some need specific items. Check IGN Wiki and MistfallDB for latest solutions.
