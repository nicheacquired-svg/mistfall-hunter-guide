Mistfall Hunter Greataxe Berserker Boss Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Hidden boss in Hallowgrove, encountered during the "Iron and Fire" questline. Guards Bell of Return fragments.

Lore

Described as "a berserker wielding a golden greataxe, golden threads drifting about its form." Connected to the Bell of Return — an ancient northern relic that guides souls back.

Location

  • Deep within Hallowgrove map
  • Questline: "Iron and Fire" must be active
  • Hallowgrove supports solo matchmaking

Boss Behavior

  • Sharpening — Periodically sharpens axe; window to attack
  • Pensive stillness — Stands in contemplation; safe window
  • Combat state — Heavy, slow swings; telegraphed

Golden threads = visual identifier.

Strategy

  • Patience — attacks are heavy but telegraphed; dodge sideways
  • Attack during sharpening — vulnerable window
  • Ranged advantage — Blackarrow/Sorcerer easier
  • Bring a tank in trios
  • Watch for PvP — Hallowgrove is PvPvE

Drops

  • Bell of Return fragments (quest progression)
  • Standard Hallowgrove loot

Tips

  • Clear nearby Corroded first
  • Have escape plan for PvP ambushes
  • Solo viable with Shadowstrix/Blackarrow
  • Don't greed — if PvP arrives, extract
Summary

Hidden Hallowgrove boss in Iron and Fire questline. Golden greataxe + golden threads. Attack during sharpening. Ranged advantage. Watch for PvP. Drops Bell of Return fragments.
