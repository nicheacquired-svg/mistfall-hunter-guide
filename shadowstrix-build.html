Mistfall Hunter Shadowstrix Build

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

The Shadowstrix is the premier assassin and solo PvP class, trading health for stealth, burst, and mobility. Two stances: Dagger (ambush/PvP) and Dual Blades (sustained/PvE).

Dagger vs Dual Blades

  • Dagger — Single-target burst. Flash Stride (blink) + Shadow Strike (delayed stun clone). Ideal for PvP ambushes.
  • Dual Blades — Sustained damage. Spinning Slash + Inspiring Impale (energy recovery). Better for PvE.

Dagger Build (PvP Ambush)

Skills

  • Flash Stride — Blink-dash for gap closing/escaping
  • Shadow Strike — Delayed shadow clone that stuns; primary setup
  • Sneak — Stealth for scouting
  • Smoke Bomb — Emergency escape

Talents

  • Seeker — Target tracking
  • Valor — Burst damage
  • Fervor — Attack speed
  • Seamless — Ability downtime
  • Elusive — Dodge
  • Strife — Combat aggression

Dual Blades Build (Sustained PvE)

Talents

  • Fervor — Attack speed for sustained DPS
  • Valor — Raw damage
  • Seeker — Target tracking
  • Seamless — Ability flow
  • Elusive — Dodge
  • Tenacious — Health (longer engagements)

Core Playstyle

  1. Sneak to scout — Assess before committing
  2. Choose angle — Approach from behind
  3. Place Shadow Strike — Time with approach
  4. Burst combo — Flash Stride in, Shadow Strike activates, full damage rotation
  5. Disengage immediately — Smoke Bomb or Flash Stride out
Build Summary

Dagger for PvP burst, Dual Blades for sustained PvE. Lowest health pool in game — every engagement is high-risk, high-reward. Master stealth positioning and disengage discipline. Was called "broken" in Beta 3; may face nerfs.
