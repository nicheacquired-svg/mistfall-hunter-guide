Mistfall Hunter Solo Mode Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 📖 Mistfall Hunter Guide

Can you play Mistfall Hunter solo? Yes, but with important caveats. Solo play is supported, but it's still a PvPvE game - you'll encounter other players even when playing alone. Here's what you need to know.

Can You Play Solo?

Yes. You can queue for matches solo without filling your squad with randoms. However, Mistfall Hunter is not an offline game and does not have a pure PvE mode. When you play solo, you enter the same PvPvE servers as squads - meaning you may face organized 3-player teams.

There is no dedicated duo queue. If you play as a pair, you'll be placed in the trio queue. You can disable "Fill Party" to avoid getting a random third teammate.

Map Support for Solo

Hallowgrove (Solo Available)

Hallowgrove supports solo matchmaking in both Normal and Chaos difficulties. This is the safer map for solo players - it has no shrinking mist (Gyldenmist) mechanic, allowing for more deliberate, methodical play. You can extract at designated points without time pressure from the circle.

Brandrgarde (Solo Coming September)

⚠ Brandrgarde Solo Not Yet Available

Brandrgarde currently only supports 3-player squads. The developers have officially confirmed that solo mode for Brandrgarde (Normal and Cataclysm difficulties) will be added in September 2026 during the mid-Season 1 update.

Brandrgarde features the Gyldenmist shrinking circle mechanic and allows extraction at any time, making it the higher-risk, higher-reward map. Once solo mode launches, it will be the preferred map for experienced solo players.

Best Solo Classes

When playing solo, survival and escape capability are paramount. The community consensus for best solo classes:

  • Shadowstrix (Dual Blades) - S tier for solo. Invisibility lets you disengage, and burst damage lets you win 1v1s. The ultimate ratting class.
  • Blackarrow (Bow) - S tier for solo. Ranged kiting keeps you safe, and charged shots apply bleed for attrition. Excellent for PvE farming.
  • Mercenary (S&S) - A tier for solo. Self-healing and blocking give you forgiveness. Good for newer solo players.
  • Withered Knight - A tier for solo. Tanky with good damage, but lacks mobility for escaping bad situations.
  • Sorcerer - A tier for solo. High damage but fragile. Requires excellent positioning and game sense.
  • Seer - B tier for solo. Designed for team support; without teammates to heal, Seer struggles. Not recommended for solo.

Solo Strategies

Ratting (Stealth Looting)

"Ratting" is a popular solo strategy: avoid combat entirely, sneak around the map collecting loot, and extract before anyone notices. Shadowstrix is the best class for this due to invisibility. The goal is to get in, grab valuables, and get out without engaging.

Third-Partying

As a solo player, wait for squads to engage each other, then clean up the survivors. This is risky but can yield high-tier loot from fallen players. Blackarrow excels at this due to ranged damage output.

Extraction Priority

When playing solo, prioritize extraction over greed. A successful extraction with mediocre loot is better than dying with a full bag. Use the Keychain and Pip's Pouch to protect your most valuable items from death penalty.

Death Penalty for Solo

If you die solo, you lose all carried items - there's no teammate to revive you. Items stored in your warehouse and Pip's Pouch are safe. Soul Form (PvE deaths only) allows you to return to your corpse for a chance at recovery, but in PvPvE, other players can loot your body.

Summary

Solo is viable on Hallowgrove now. Brandrgarde solo arrives September 2026. Best solo classes: Shadowstrix and Blackarrow. Play stealthy, prioritize extraction, and protect key items with Pip's Pouch.
